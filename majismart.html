The Problem

Water is everywhere —
reliable, safe water is not

67% No Piped Water

Infrastructure Gap

Only 33% of Kenyan households have piped access (KNBS/KDHS 2022). The rest rely on vendors, boreholes, or tankers — with zero reliability or quality guarantees.

Trust
Gap Safety Uncertainty

Quality Anxiety

Even when utilities supply treated water, most households still boil or treat — driving persistent demand for delivered branded water with clear provenance.

Zero Digital Dispatch

No Unified Platform

No platform connects households to vetted vendors with real-time tracking, quality controls, and flexible M-Pesa payment. The market is fragmented and analogue.

Go-to-Market

Estate-first. Office-second.
Lock channels before scaling supply.

🏘️
Residential Estates

Partner with property managers. Bulk deals + scheduled refills. Captive audience of 50–500 households per estate — predictable volume, low CAC.

🏢
Offices & Co-working

3–10 bottles/week per office. High repeat, predictable logistics, easy SLA commitments. B2B channel with high retention.

🏪
Retail Resellers (Dukas)

Kiosks and dukas as micro-pickup points. Reduces last-100m cost. Expands supply map without additional rider overhead.
